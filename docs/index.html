Your loadout

Buy weapons with credits in the hangar, then switch between them mid-fight. Everything you unlock stays unlocked.

Primary

  • Laser CannonFast twin lasers that pierce one target.
  • Plasma GunSlow heavy bolts that splash on impact.
  • Machine GunLow damage, absurd fire rate.
  • Pulse CannonFive-way fan for crowds.

Secondary

  • MissilesDumb-fire pair, big boom.
  • Homing MissilesFind the nearest target on their own.
  • EMP BombStuns enemies and erases their bullets.
  • Cluster RocketsBurst into eight bomblets.

Ultimate

  • Orbital StrikeSatellite lances hit every enemy on screen.
  • Energy WaveExpanding shockwave that deletes bullets.
  • Black Hole GeneratorA singularity that devours enemies.
  • Drone SwarmSix drones fight beside you for twelve seconds.

In the field

Seven ships, two of them legendary

Start in the balanced Vanguard. Earn the Swiftwing for speed, the Bulwark for armour, the Longbow for range, the Xel'Thar Seed for regenerating shields, and two legendary hulls for finishing the war.
