# Controls Guide

## PC (keyboard + mouse)

| Action | Keys |
|---|---|
| Move ship | **W A S D** or arrow keys |
| Aim | Mouse – the ship rotates toward the cursor |
| Fire primary | **Left mouse button** (hold), or **J** |
| Secondary weapon (missiles etc.) | **Right mouse button** or **F** |
| Boost | **Left Shift** (hold) – drains energy |
| Ultimate ability | **Space** (needs 100% charge) |
| Select primary weapon | **1 2 3 4** (unlocked weapons only) |
| Cycle primary / secondary | **E** / **Q**, or mouse wheel up / down |
| Pause | **Esc** or **P** |

Debug (Editor and Development builds only): `F1` god mode, `F2` destroy all enemies, `F3` +5000 credits.

## Gamepad

| Action | Button |
|---|---|
| Move | Left stick |
| Aim | Right stick (also fires when pushed past 50%) |
| Fire | Right trigger |
| Secondary | Right bumper |
| Boost | Left trigger or L3 |
| Ultimate | Y / Triangle |
| Cycle primary / secondary | X / Square, Left bumper |
| Pause | Start |

## Mobile (touch)

The HUD shows touch controls automatically on Android/iOS, or on desktop when a touch is detected (toggle "Show touch controls on desktop" in Settings to force them).

- **Left half of the screen** – floating virtual joystick. Touch anywhere and drag; the stick appears under your finger and follows it.
  - Settings → **Touch: drag to move** switches this to direct drag: the ship moves with your finger 1:1 (with a 1.25× boost so you don't cover the ship).
- **FIRE** (large red) – hold to shoot. Enable **Auto-fire** in Settings to shoot constantly.
- **MSL** – secondary weapon.
- **BOOST** – hold for speed.
- **ULT** – ultimate when charged (the purple bar in the bottom-right fills up).
- **WPN / SEC** – cycle primary / secondary weapon.
- **⏸** (top centre) – pause.

Aiming on touch is automatic: the ship targets the nearest enemy in front of it.

## HUD legend

- **Top-left**: Hull (green), Shield (blue, regenerates after 2.5 s without damage), Energy (yellow, used by boost), lives, current primary weapon.
- **Top-right**: score, credits earned this run (+ bank), combo multiplier.
- **Top-centre**: current objective. During a boss fight the boss health (red) and shield (blue) bars appear here.
- **Bottom-right**: missiles, secondary weapon + cooldown bar, ultimate charge.

## Tips

- Shields absorb damage first; stop taking hits for a moment and they regenerate.
- Killing enemies quickly builds a **combo** that multiplies score.
- Bombers explode when destroyed and damage everything around them, including other enemies.
- Boss shields only drop once every glowing **generator** on the hull is destroyed; then aim for the bright **core** for double damage.
- EMP bombs and the Energy Wave delete enemy bullets – save them for ring walls and spirals.
- Credits earned during a run are banked when the sector is cleared; if you die you keep half.
